A harbour city of smugglers, dock crews and bent officials

PORT MERIDIAN

Jae Corin owes the Salt Union money. Mara Voss offers work. Steal cars, run cargo, fight, and shake the Meridian Port Police off your tail.

Controls

  • W A S D / Arrows move   Shift sprint / handbrake
  • E enter or leave a car, talk, pick up   F punch
  • Mouse aim   Click shoot (with a pistol)   1 / 2 weapon
  • P / Esc pause   M mute   Enter start
